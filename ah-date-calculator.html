How the AH year overlaps the human calendar
The AH calendar year changes in November, not January. November and December belong to the newly started AH year. January through October remain in that same AH year even though the human year number has increased.
The Hatching: November 1859 marks the beginning of 0 AH. December 1859 is therefore December 0 AH. November 1860 begins 1 AH.
